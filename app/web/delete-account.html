Delete your FamChat account

Last updated: November 16, 2026

FamChat is a private, invite-only messenger for one family. You can delete your FamChat account at any time, in any of these ways.

How to delete your account

  1. In the app: open Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm. This works on any of your signed-in devices, on every platform.
  2. Ask a family admin: an admin can open Settings → Family members, choose you and tap Remove from family. This deletes your account exactly as above.
  3. If you've lost all your devices and can't reach an admin, request deletion at github.com/darkharasho/famchat/issues. Don't include personal information in the issue: just ask for an account deletion, and we'll reply there to arrange confirming who you are privately before deleting the account.

If you're your family's only admin and others are still in the family, make someone else an admin first (Settings → Family members → Make admin), so the family isn't left without one.

What is deleted

Deletion happens immediately, as soon as you confirm (or the admin removes you):

  • Your account and name. Your name is replaced with "Deleted user", and your admin role and admin recovery code are removed. Your account can't be restored or signed in to.
  • Your devices. Every device is signed out; its device name, sign-in tokens and push notification token are deleted. Pending sign-in links and invites you created stop working.
  • Your chats. You leave every chat, and the remaining members' apps switch to new encryption keys that you don't have.
  • Everything you sent: the encrypted messages, reactions, photos and voice messages you sent are deleted from the FamChat server and its storage.
  • On your devices: the app deletes its keys, messages and cached media at once on the device you deleted from, and on your other devices as soon as they're next online.

What is kept

  • Copies on other family members' devices. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so the server can't reach copies other members' apps already downloaded. They stay on those devices until deleted there.
  • Messages others sent in chats you were in stay with them.
  • An anonymous placeholder: a "Deleted user" entry with a random account ID, and your public encryption keys and those of your old devices (no device names), so other members' apps can still check the signatures on messages they already have. Public keys can't be used to read anything.
  • A log entry recording that the account was deleted, when, and whether you or an admin did it (by account ID).
  • Database backups: the server's database provider (Cloudflare) keeps a point-in-time history of the database for up to 30 days for disaster recovery. After that, deleted data is gone from it too. Deleted photos and voice messages aren't kept in any backup.
