FamChat privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

FamChat is a private, invite-only messenger for one family. Nobody can sign up on their own: an admin in the family invites each member. App-store reviewers may get separate reviewer accounts from an admin; those can only see and chat with each other, never the family or its chats, and an admin can remove them at any time. This policy covers the FamChat apps (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux and the web) and the FamChat server at famchat.dev.

Messages, photos and voice messages are end-to-end encrypted

Everything you send in a chat is encrypted on your device before it leaves it, with keys that only the devices of the chat's members hold. The server stores and relays the encrypted data but cannot read your messages, see your photos or listen to your voice messages.

What the server can see

  • Your account: the name you're shown under, whether you're an admin, and your public keys.
  • Your devices: each device's name (for example "Pixel 8"), its platform, when it was added and last seen, and, if you allow notifications, a push token from Google or Apple.
  • Chats: who is in each chat, group chat names, and when each encrypted message, photo or voice message was sent, by whom and how large it is.
  • Notifications: to show "New message from <name>", the server sends the sender's name and the group's name to Google's or Apple's notification service. It never sends message contents.
  • Network addresses: when a new device asks to sign in, its IP address is kept with that short-lived request to limit repeated attempts. It isn't stored with your account.
  • Account events: a log of devices added or removed, invites and account resets, which family admins can view.

What FamChat doesn't do

  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Your data is never sold or shared, except with the services that run FamChat (below).
  • FamChat doesn't read your contacts or your location.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos you send and to scan the code that signs in a new device.
  • Microphone: to record voice messages, only while you're recording one.
  • Photos: to pick photos you choose to send.
  • Notifications: to tell you about new messages.

Services FamChat uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the server, the database and the encrypted media.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service deliver notifications.

Keeping and deleting data

Encrypted messages and media are kept so your devices can sync your history. Removing a device from Settings revokes it and deletes its push token.

You can delete your account at any time: in the app under Settings → Delete my account, or by asking a family admin to remove you. Deletion is immediate. It signs out all your devices and deletes your name, device names, sign-in and push tokens, and the encrypted messages, photos and voice messages you sent. What stays is an anonymous "Deleted user" placeholder with your public keys (so others' apps can still check signatures), a log entry that the account was deleted, copies already on other members' devices, and, for up to 30 days, the database provider's recovery history. Details and other ways to ask: famchat.dev/delete-account.html.

Children

FamChat is for members of one family, who may include children invited by a parent who is an admin. It contains no ads and nothing is shared with third parties for marketing.
