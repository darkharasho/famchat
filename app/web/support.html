FamChat support

FamChat is a private, invite-only messenger for one family. Members join with an invite link from a family admin; there is no public sign-up.

Contact

Email support@famchat.dev, or open an issue at github.com/darkharasho/famchat/issues.

Common questions

How do I join? Ask a family admin for an invite link, open FamChat and paste it on the first screen.

How do I add another device? On the new device choose Sign in with another device. On a device where you already use FamChat, open Devices → Add a device → Show a code, and scan or enter that code on the new device.

I'm not getting notifications. Check that notifications are allowed for FamChat in your device settings, then open the app once so it can register again.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Settings → Delete my account. See famchat.dev/delete-account.html for other ways.

Privacy

Messages, photos and voice messages are end-to-end encrypted. Read the privacy policy.
